### 2.1 THE SPIDER WEB COMMAND LATTICE (how ships hold together)

**2.1.2 — THE THREE-ARM-LENGTH LAW (the owner's assertion, carried verbatim in form): no one in the fleet is ever more than three arm lengths away from someone who can do something.** Three arm lengths is the web's smallest knot interval: within reach of hand, sensor, or actuator — someone who can act NOW, not someone who must ask.
