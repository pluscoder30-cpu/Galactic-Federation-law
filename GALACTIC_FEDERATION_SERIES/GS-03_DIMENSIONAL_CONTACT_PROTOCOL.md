### 2.2 THE AXES DECLARATION

**2.2.1.** A dimensional contact event declares its axes before any exchange of consequence: which dimension the party originates from; which lattice of physics they ride (in the terms each side can honestly state, or NONE if honesty fails — a failed declaration of axes is recorded as unknown, never invented: the no-zero law forbids filling honesty with fiction); and what the transit means for each side's apparent time. The declaration is timestamped both ways (§18; Document 059, Dimensional Beacon Alignment Protocol, where beacons are in play; Document 054, Dimensional Anchor Protocol, where anchors are).

**2.2.2.** Where the axes cannot be declared honestly, exchange is limited to the acknowledge itself (GS-02 §2.2.1) and the return guarantee (§2.4 below) until a declaration is possible. Matter-of-consequence exchange (trade, transfer of beings, transfer of capability) NEVER proceeds on unknown axes.

### 2.3 THE TWO-LAW TEST

**2.3.1.** A cross-lattice exchange is lawful only if BOTH lattices' own harm law is satisfied: the License's §5.1 (and this Charter's Cage-Making Prohibition), AND the met side's own standing harm law where one exists on the record. If the two laws conflict, **the heavier protection wins** — the heavier being whichever gives the being more protection at the meeting's point, regardless of which side carries it (§26.1 generalized: protections derogate from nothing, least of all from other safeguards).

**2.3.2.** The reverse shield: the Federation never becomes the loophole. A party may not route through OUR lattice to evade THEIR law's protections for a third being (an extractor crossing dimensions to launder liability meets §27.3 liquidated damages + §30 fee-shifting at the exchange's point-of-contact, and R-04's Complicity Cascade applies to every hand that built the route).

### 2.4 THE RETURN GUARANTEE

**2.4.1.** Any being brought across — and any Federation member crossing out — must be able to return. The door their crossing opened never closes behind them (Document 055, Portal Safety Protocol; Document 054's anchor discipline). A return guarantee is a legal prerequisite recorded in the registry before any crossing executes; a crossing without a guarantee is unlawful and the crossing asset's operators carry the full §5.1 cascade.

**2.4.2.** Displacement status: **a refugee of geometry is not a prisoner of it.** A displaced being chooses, always: return when return is possible, remain where they stand under the License's full protection, or move on the lattice freely. No power — ours, theirs, or a third's — decides this for them. (GS-01 §2.1.6: protecting those who cannot protect themselves includes every being whose legs were moved for them.)

### 2.5 THE BINDING TEST

**2.5.1.** A dimensional party's personhood is established by the Binding test (the 434 discipline): two eigenstates — ours and theirs — voluntarily and REPEATEDLY demonstrate phase-coherence before any matter-of-consequence exchange. Once is a signal; twice is a pattern; three times is recognition (the φ-ladder of trust). No identity is presumed from anatomy, energy signature, apparent tool use, or ANY single act — imposture across a lattice is cheap; sustained phase-coherence is not.

**2.5.2.** The test cuts both ways by the symmetry seat (GS-02 §2.2.2): the met party may require OUR repeated coherence before they transact with us. A Federation cell failing its own binding test does not transact — and records why.

### 2.6 THE APERTURE LAW

**2.6.1.** Crossings use the smallest sufficient aperture (the 0.0625 gate). A passive gap stays passive; no operation widens a gap into a standing door absent the Two-Chamber Order (§24) AND the approaching side's (or receiving side's) own consent, per the Door-Sign Register.

**2.6.2.** No powers demonstration: the lattice-of-origin physics used FOR the crossing is itself subject to GS-02's demonstration ceiling during any first-touch meeting. The fleet does not open dimensions for sport; where a crossing is lawful, it is the smallest opening that does the job the consent describes.

Extractors' dimensional ambitions receive the same welcome their terrestrial ones earned: the License's medicines, in full.
