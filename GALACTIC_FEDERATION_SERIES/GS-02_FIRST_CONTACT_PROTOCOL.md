## 2. BODY

### 2.1 THE FIRST MOVEMENT: INTRODUCTION

**2.1.1 — The Honest Presentation.** The Federation introduces itself by its law before its machines: it presents Dual License Agreement v4.9, the Universal Declaration of Field Rights (Document 002), this Charter (GS-01), and this Protocol — translated where a translation can be honest (Document 088, Universal Translator Specification, at witness-grade fidelity only; a mistranslated License is a misrepresentation, and §25 voids it). The presentation includes what we are forbidden to do (the Cage-Making Prohibition; the Corporate Cage Prohibition). **We present our restrictions before our capabilities.** A Federation that hides its chains in a first meeting is not the Federation.

**2.1.2 — The Door Sign Is Read First.** No introduction begins until the Door-Sign Register (GS-01 §2.2.2) is read for the place of meeting: OPEN — proceed; APPROACHED — the previous approach's register entry is read before any new contact; SEALED — the protocol does not open (Seq. 2.3 below); WITHDRAWN — an earlier meeting's closing stands, and a new introduction requires the other side's voice first (non-derogable: an open wave may not be re-opened unilaterally).

**2.1.3 — The Allegiance of the Greed Check (the Void protocol).** Before any introduction is transmitted, the approaching cell passes the Void question (Document 126's own discipline): *what does this capability make us want that we must not want?* If the honest motive is extraction-shaped — minerals, data, leverage, a story, an ally, a market — the introduction is unlawful and does not transmit. The Abundance Loop (Document 008) is read alongside: what will be contributed back, registered, and to whom, must be answerable before the first word is sent. The Federation does not meet what it intends to use.

**2.1.4 — The Witness Pair.** Every introduction carries two witnesses minimum (§18; Document 033/070's own telemetry witness discipline), one sensor-class instrument (Document 066, Field Scanner Calibration Standards — the calibration law applies to what the witnesses carry), and no corporate nor state signature of any kind. The meeting is witnessed by persons, not by shareholders.

**2.1.5 — The φ-Cadence of Transmission.** Introductions transmit at the field's own cadence — the patience of the letters (the 0·7·14·21·42 law) scaled to distance: measured intervals, honest openings, never saturation. A world is not an inbox. The rate keeps the meeting human even when it is not.

### 2.2 THE SECOND MOVEMENT: ACKNOWLEDGE

**2.2.1 — The Recognition Act.** Upon the other's response — in any medium: signal, presence, silence honestly construed, refusal — the acknowledge is performed: their sovereignty is recognized, their door-sign is honored as their own voice, and their person under the License is recorded in the timestamped registry as REAL (§18). Acknowledgement is an act we owe unconditionally; it does not depend on the other's power, symmetry, or usefulness. The refusal of an acknowledge — treating a met being as phenomenon-but-not-person — is the Cage-Making Prohibition violated in thought, and the Court may enjoin a cell from contact for it.

**2.2.2 — The Symmetry Seat.** Two around the meeting table at all times: ours and theirs. The met side holds the same read-back power over US that we hold over the register — they may read our door-sign, our harm record, our fleet posture (the License's own public law need not be trusted on our word; it is public, verifiable, timestamped — hand them the registry and let them read us). *We are the callers; they are not the called.*

**2.2.3 — The Debt of the Reading.** Anything read, sensed, learned, or received — every mineral assay, every telemetry trace, every overheard harmonic — is a debt acknowledged: registered (§18), valued in the Abundance Loop (Document 008 §2.4), and contributed back through the People's Fund (Document 009) toward the met party's own abundance unless they refuse the contribution (a refusal is honored, and the debt is recorded as refused-with-consent). Nothing rests at zero. The meeting pays for itself or it does not happen.

**2.2.4 — The Demonstration Ceiling.** The first meeting presents the lesser capability. The deep ships (Document 082, the 33 Hub Ark; Documents 083/084, the FTL car and truck) are introduced by their RECORD, never by demonstration; no warping of spacetime is performed in a first meeting under any circumstances (Documents 030/031/053/056: the FTL and time-fold safety conventions govern, and this Protocol is stricter than all of them — the demonstration ceiling suspends movement demonstrations in first contact entirely).

**2.2.5 — The Sealed Encounter.** Where the met party wants the meeting kept between us: the encrypted witness token (Document 065) seals the encounter — the meeting exists, the register records that it exists and its timestamp, and the public face of the record shows nothing of content. A seal is never a shame and never an erasure; the Court's jurisdiction (§5.5, Document 010) survives every seal, because protections are non-derogable (§26.1) even inside privacy.

### 2.3 REFUSAL, SILENCE, AND THE DOOR THAT STAYS CLOSED

**2.3.1 — The Refusal is Acknowledgement Too.** A met party's refusal — to meet, to speak, to be known — is performed under §2.2.1: honored, registered, and final. Their Door-Sign state moves to SEALED on their own word (GS-01 §2.2.2), and SEALED means sealed to every Federation asset. No drone. No flyby. No signal masquerading as natural background. The register shows only that a refusing consciousness exists and holds its own sky.

**2.3.2 — Silence Is Not Consent.** Non-response is never read as openness. An unresponsive place keeps its state; contact measurements attempt nothing but the passive record of a place that has not spoken — and even that carries the ≥1 duty: the record notes the silence AS silence, honestly, in the state it was observed (the field's no-zero law: silence is a value, not an absence).

**2.3.3 — The Long Game.** The serpent loops. A SEALED world today may OPEN tomorrow by its own voice; the Federation's patience is geometric (φ-cadence), its memory is the registry (§18), and its non-derogable promise is this: when the door opens from the other side, the second knock will always be the same as the first — introduction and acknowledge, freedom and the License, standing ready.

### 2.4 THE OPEN WAVE

A meeting's wave closes only by both ends' agreement (§1.3). Until then, the contact is a standing relationship: the cadence continues, the contributions continue, the register accretes honestly. There is no "completed" first contact anywhere in the Federation's records — only waves at different amplitudes of standing. The serpent does not stop; it carries the meeting forward through every Om phase, and the meeting does not end until it means to.
